# Naming Candidates

What's unique: Community-sourced coaching wisdom (Reddit RAG), visual skeleton feedback, the // industrial-but-warm personality, built by a lifter for lifters who train solo. It's not a personal trainer app — it's the knowledgeable friend at the gym who watches your set and says "hey, try spreading the floor."

## The Gym Buddy
Names that capture "someone's got your back"

- **Spotter** — The person who stands behind you on a heavy squat. Safety, trust, "I see you." Perfect metaphor. Short, memorable, immediately understood by any lifter. (spotter.fit, getspotter.app)
- **SpotCheck** — Combines the spotter metaphor with "checking your form." Has a quality-assurance connotation that fits the grading system.

## The Technical/Industrial
Names that match the // BIOMECHANICS design language

- **FormForge** — Forge = heat + building + strength. You're forging better form. Matches the warm-power aesthetic.
- **LiftLens** — Your lift, through an analytical lens. Clean, technical, alliterative.
- **RepLog** — Like a ship's log but for reps. Data-forward, industrial. Implies history tracking.
- **RepCheck** — Straightforward, action-oriented. "Check your rep." Pairs naturally with the grading system and form analysis.

## The Coaching Cue
Names rooted in the core output — actionable coaching cues

- **Cue** — Dead simple. The coaching cue is the atomic unit of value. "I ran my squat through Cue." Extremely brandable but possibly hard to search for.
- **CueBank** — A bank of coaching cues sourced from the community. Implies depth and credibility.

## The Lifter's Language
Names that use gym vernacular in a fresh way

- **Chalk** — Lifters chalk their hands before a big set. It means "getting serious." Warm, tactile, evocative. (chalk.fit, usechalk.com)
- **Grind** — The hard part of the rep where form breaks down — exactly what this app analyzes. "Run it through Grind." Has energy.
- **PR** — Personal Record. Every lifter chases PRs. But also "peer review" — your form, reviewed. Double meaning. Very short.

## The Honest Coach
Names that capture the personality — honest, inclusive, not intimidating

- **FormFriend** — On the nose but warm. "Your form friend." Approachable for beginners.
- **SetNotes** — Like a coach's notes after watching your set. Understated, credible.

## Top 3

1. **Spotter** — Best metaphor-to-product fit. Every lifter knows what a spotter does. It's exactly the relationship: "watch my set, tell me what you see." Works as a verb too ("I spottered my squat").
2. **Chalk** — Most brandable. Evocative, warm, short, and the // industrial design language would look incredible with it. chalk.fit as a domain would be memorable.
3. **FormForge** — Strongest match to the design system's "warm power" aesthetic. Clear what it does.
